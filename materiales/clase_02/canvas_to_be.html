La conjugación

SujetoAfirmativoNegativoPregunta
II am (I'm)I'm notAm I…?
youYou are (you're)You aren'tAre you…?
heHe is (he's)He isn'tIs he…?
sheShe is (she's)She isn'tIs she…?
itIt is (it's)It isn'tIs it…?
weWe are (we're)We aren'tAre we…?
theyThey are (they're)They aren'tAre they…?
Respuesta corta Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.
Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.
Ojo con esto En la respuesta afirmativa no se contrae: se dice «Yes, I am», nunca «Yes, I'm». Y en la pregunta el verbo va antes del sujeto: «Are you from Chile?».
