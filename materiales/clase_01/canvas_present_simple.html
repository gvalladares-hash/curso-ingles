How an English sentence works

Three parts in a fixed order, one ‑s for he / she / it, and two small words that open every question.

1 The skeleton — every sentence has three parts

SUBJECT + VERB + COMPLEMENT

Subject
María
who does the action — never omitted in English
+
Verb
sells
the action, always in second place
+
Complement
churrascas
what, where or when — the rest of the idea
María|sells|churrascas at the fair.
We|work|in Samo Alto.
Tourists|visit|the valley in summer.
Spanish can hide the subject — «vendo churrascas». English cannot: say I sell churrascas. The subject is the first thing the listener hears.
2 Third person singular

He / she / it → the verb takes ‑s

One person or one thing — not I, not you, not they.

I work→She works
You sell→María sells
They visit→The tourist visits
We help→He helps
Spelling: work → works · watch → watches · go → goes
study → studies · but have → has (She has a shop.)
  • ✗María sell churrascas.
  • ✓María sells churrascas.
