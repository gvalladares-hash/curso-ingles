At the Feria Costumbrista

María, vendedora de churrascas del valle · Sophie, turista australiana · Samo Alto, miércoles, cuatro de la tarde

1

El diálogo

Léelo dos veces: primero a velocidad normal, después lento. Fíjate en las fórmulas fijas: I'm…, I'm from…, Nice to meet you, You're welcome.

El diálogo completo, 57 segundos. Para proyectar, versión 16:9.

MARÍA
Good afternoon! Welcome to the Feria Costumbrista. I'm María.
SOPHIE
Good afternoon! Nice to meet you, María. I'm Sophie.
MARÍA
Nice to meet you, Sophie. Are you from Chile?
SOPHIE
No, I'm from Australia. I'm from Sydney.
MARÍA
Welcome to the Valle del Río Hurtado! How do you spell your name?
SOPHIE
S-O-P-H-I-E. And “churrasca”?
MARÍA
C-H-U-R-R-A-S-C-A. Ha! Are you looking for something?
SOPHIE
I'm just looking at the products. They're beautiful.
MARÍA
Thank you! Churrascas! They're flat bread from the embers, a family recipe. With goat cheese, pebre or avocado.
SOPHIE
Goat cheese from the valley?
MARÍA
Yes! Goat cheese from my farm, in Samo Alto. And a cup of tea or fresh juice.
SOPHIE
One churrasca with goat cheese, please. And a cup of tea.
MARÍA
Very good. One churrasca with goat cheese and one tea. Thank you!
SOPHIE
Thank you, María.
MARÍA
You're welcome. Enjoy your churrasca!

El verbo to be en presente

Tres formas, una sola idea: am / is / are. Lo que cambia es el sujeto, no el verbo.

Persona Afirmativo Negativo Pregunta
I I'm from Samo Alto. I'm not from Ovalle. Am I late?
You You're welcome. You aren't late. Are you from Chile?
He / She / It She's a tourist.
It's a family recipe.		 She isn't from Chile.
It isn't expensive.		 Is she from Australia?
Is it fresh?
We We're at the feria. We aren't closed. Are we ready?
They They're beautiful. They aren't sweet. Are they from the valley?

Forma completa: I am · you are · he is · she is · it is · we are · they are.
Contracción: I'm · you're · he's · she's · it's · we're · they're.
Negación: I'm not · you aren't (you're not) · she isn't (she's not) · they aren't (they're not).
Respuesta corta: Yes, I am. / No, I'm not. · Yes, she is. / No, she isn't. · Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.

Ejercicio 2 · Negativo

Completa con 'm not, isn't o aren't.

  1. I from Argentina.
  2. Sophie from Chile.
  3. The churrascas sweet.
  4. We closed today.
  5. The tea cold.
  6. You a tourist.
Clave 1. I'm not · 2. isn't · 3. aren't · 4. aren't · 5. isn't · 6. aren't
También se aceptan las formas completas: am not, is not, are not.
