Las ocho preguntas

Wh-PreguntaRespuesta
WhatWhat do you make?I make bags.
WhereWhere are you from?I'm from Canada.
WhenWhen do you open?At nine.
WhoWho is with you?My daughter.
WhyWhy do you work here?Because I love the valley.
WhichWhich bag do you like?This one.
HowHow do you make them?By hand.
How muchHow much is it?Twelve thousand pesos.
El orden Wh- + auxiliar + sujeto + verbo
Where are you from? · What do you make?
Ojo con esto How much pregunta precio. How many pregunta cantidad: How many wallets? Five.

Ejercicio 1 completa con la palabra correcta

1 do you make? — Bags and wallets.
2 are you from? — From Canada.
3 do you open? — At nine.
4 is with you? — My daughter.
5 is this wallet? — Twelve thousand.
6 bag do you like? — The black one.
7 do you make them? — By hand.
8 do you work here? — Because I love it.

Ejercicio 2 ordena y escribe la pregunta

2do · what · you · make

3is · how · much · it

4you · who · with · is
